# Proposal: v0.4.3 — measured per-draw routing (2026-09-16)

Written the morning after v0.4.2's first full live day. Every number below was measured
today or read from the repo's own gate/episode records; hypotheses are labelled.

## Step 1 executed (measured 2026-09-16, evening — the per-draw measurement is DONE)

Panel: 11,688 screen+refine episodes + 1,536 deep episodes + 1,535 peer-vs-peer
reference episodes, all against `opponents/v0_4_2.py` self-play (the honest chassis-peer
proxy), paired seeds, both seats. Machinery: `scripts/v043_common.py` (forced-tape
variants), `scripts/v043_panel.py` (screen → refine → validate → deep),
`scripts/v043_endgame.py` (Step 3), `scripts/v043_fills.py` (mechanism check).
Deliverable: `logs/v043_draw_table.csv` (pair → route, in-sample + held-out evidence,
`changed` and `conservative` columns).

Two measurement discoveries that changed the method (both load-bearing for any
future panel work):

1. **The pass-vs-pass draw table does NOT transfer to chassis games** (0/12 seeds
   matched). Chassis agents occupy tiles from step 0, which diverges the weed-spawn
   RNG. All stratification therefore uses `logs/seed_draws_chassis.csv` (2,300+
   chassis-vs-chassis enumerations, every pair ≥24 seeds), and every episode's
   observed pair is read from the episode itself (`local_arena` now records
   `observed_pair`).
2. **The engine is seat-asymmetric on some seeds**: identical agents do NOT tie
   (~5/130 self-play episodes, seat-0-favored ±$1–7k, deterministic). The mechanism
   is `_process_market`'s per-unit lockstep committing in player order — with
   sell-spam strategies, seat 0's SELLs move the shared inventory before seat 1's
   identical SELLs clear. Consequences implemented: anchors validate against a
   peer-vs-peer REFERENCE (forced-incumbent episodes must reproduce it seat-for-seat
   — all 9,728 screen records do), and every tape's evidence is the PAIRED DIFF vs
   the incumbent cell on the same (seed, seat) keys, which cancels the asymmetry.
   This also re-explains part of the identical-agent converged-WR noise floor and
   makes both-seat pairing mandatory (it always was in this repo).

Results:

- **60/64 assignments change** under the greedy rule (mean paired diff > 0 on ≥10
  common seeds). In-sample frequency-weighted EV +$1,636/game.
- **Held-out (seeds 12–24, untouched by selection): +$1,042/game** for the full
  table — below the +$1,500 gate; the winner's-curse retention is ~64% (max-of-4
  selection on 12 seeds, exactly the "offline gates overstate" lesson).
- **Conservative subset** (in-sample filter only: mean ≥ $1,000 AND ≥10/12 positive
  diffs): 20 flips, **held-out EV +$1,038/game with 19/20 pairs retaining positive**.
  Nearly all the EV, one fifth of the surface, and every assignment is
  out-of-sample validated. Largest edges (held-out, both-seat): FARMERS_MARKET×2
  104→112 +$17.6k (12/0), BAKERY/FARMERS_MARKET 104→103 +$6.9k (12/0), PIZZA_SHOP/
  ICE_CREAM_SHOP 122→120 +$6.5k (12/0), ICE_CREAM_SHOP/PIZZA_SHOP 108→120 +$5.8k
  (11/1), PET_CAFE/FARMERS_MARKET 117→112 +$5.7k (12/0), YARN_STORE/PET_CAFE 10→11
  +$3.6k (12/0).
- Mechanism verified as route-choice on the biggest flip (FARMERS_MARKET×2: tape
  112 sells 80 TOMATO units at $192 the incumbent tape never produces, plus better
  MILK timing — `scripts/v043_fills.py`, `logs/v043_fills.log`).
- **Composition check passed**: the composed table-variant reproduces its
  constituent cells' episodes exactly (452/452 deep episodes match reference or
  candidate cells — the table composes with zero interaction).

**Step 3 (parallel) executed — the day-27 endgame is MEASURED-CORRECT.** Route 2 vs
candidates on 60 paired seeds, both seats: route 2 (incumbent) 2W-57T-1L; route 5
2W-14T-44L (mean +$104 but 44 losses); route 1 1W-59L (−$925). The class-shared
endgame choice is right for engine 1.32.7; stop suspecting it. No endgame change
folds into v0.4.3.

**Decision-gate verdict**: the premise did NOT die (60 flips, mechanism verified,
composition exact), but the full-table EV (+$1,042 held-out) misses the +$1,500
bar. Per the gate, do not ship the 60-flip table. The **conservative 20-flip subset**
is the honest ship candidate: EV +$1,038 held-out at a fifth of the surface, every
flip individually out-of-sample positive (19/20). Step 2 should build v0.4.3 with
the conservative table (and only it), gate it head-to-head against v0.4.2 on ≥100
paired seeds draw-stratified, and let the live read decide. If the conservative
table's live read is parity, stop shipping table tweaks — the remaining margin is
then likely outside the routing table entirely (midgame reset, per the proposal's
Step 4).

Three readings:

1. **v0.4.1's EXP240 graft was live-neutral, and that is a verdict on the class, not the
   graft.** The three submissions' converged WRs (29.4 / 30.8 / 37.5) have overlapping
   CIs, and v0.4.2 — byte-identical to v0.4.1 — sits 7 points above it. That 7-point
   spread between identical agents *is* our noise floor at n≈36. The graft did what it
   was gated to do (66.7% vs v0.4.0 offline; complete parity per draw), but parity per
   draw was never going to beat the field per draw — everyone in the chassis class now
   routes ~41 tapes by day-6 shop pair.
2. **We are measuring the right thing but at the class ceiling.** At ~30–37% converged
   WR inside a ~2500–2630 band, every peer game is a near-coin-flip decided by margins.
   The +EV inside the class is small, per-draw, and requires *measured* per-draw edges —
   which is exactly what our unique tooling (paired-seed arena, draw table, realized-
   revenue instrumentation) can produce and the public kernels cannot.
3. **The top-10 churned 40% overnight.** The 2960+ class is still separating. The gap
   from 2590 to ~3000 is not one trick; it is compounding small per-draw edges plus
   possibly a different endgame/midgame treatment. Our one structural difference from
   the public class (the day-27 switch to route 2) is *shared* with the metacounter, so
   it is not a differentiator — but it is also the only authored-for-this-engine code
   path we have not re-validated against 1.32.7 behaviour (gate below).

## Why per-draw measured routing is the highest-EV next step

- The router currently maps the 64 day-6 shop pairs to a **static** table inherited
  from EXP240's author (and from v0.4.0 for YARN pairs). Nobody in the class measured
  those assignments against the current field or engine 1.32.7 — they are a prior, not
  a measurement. The metacounter's own `_R110_OLD_SHOPS` table (all zeros except YARN
  pairs) shows these tables get rewritten per release.
- We own the exact prerequisites the assignment needs and the field does not:
  the seed→draw table (`logs/seed_draws.csv`, extended by `scripts/enumerate_draws.py`),
  the paired-seed arena (`local_arena.py`, both-seat discipline per repo lesson), and
  the draw-stratified runner (`scripts/draw_stratified.py`, built for exactly this gate).
- v0.4.1's own gate data says the portfolio *differs* per draw: excluding ties it beat
  v0.4.0 87% — ties concentrated where both sides replay identical tapes. A per-pair
  re-assignment attacks exactly the pairs where the current table's choice is a guess.
- Offline gates transfer at ~50% sign accuracy when they measure *whole-version deltas
  on 30 seeds* (five live failures, README). But this change is *router-table-only* —
  same tapes, same layers, no engine behaviour change — and per-draw effects are much
  larger than the noise the five failures taught us to respect (v0.4.1's per-draw
  held-out gate was +$1,091 with 37W-20T-3L; per-draw deltas were ±$5.7k worst-case).

## The plan

### Step 1 — build the per-draw measurement (days 1–2, offline, no submission)

For each of the 64 day-6 shop pairs, run a paired-seed panel of our candidate routes
(v0.4.1's 13 + EXP240's 29 = 42-tape portfolio) **against the strongest available
opponent snapshot of the field class** (`opponents/v0_4_2.py` self-play is the honest
proxy for chassis peers; add `scratch/public_kernels/metacounter_agent.py` and
`lynnsakurai_submit_candidate.py` as the second/third opponents):

- Seeds: draw-stratified via `scripts/enumerate_draws.py` (extend `--count` so every
  pair has ≥8 seeds; 64 pairs × 8 = 512 enumerations ≈ 8 min at 0.9 s/ep), then
  paired-seed episodes per pair (both seats, alternating — the repo's measured
  role-asymmetry lesson). ~64 pairs × 42 routes × 8 seeds × 2 seats is too big;
  prune to the 3 strongest tapes per pair from a cheap first pass (see Step 2's
  greedy table-build), then deep-evaluate only the table the greedy pass proposes.
- Metric per pair: mean cash delta and W/T/L vs the peer, plus realized-revenue
  decomposition on the losing pairs (`scripts/analyse_market_fills.py`) to verify the
  loss mechanism is route-choice and not noise.
- Output: `logs/v043_draw_table.csv` — pair → route, with the panel evidence attached,
  and a *changed-from-EXP240* column (expect ~15–30 of 64 assignments to flip; the
  metacounter's own all-zero fallback suggests the author's table was tuned for a
  different engine version).

**Decision gate:** if ≤5 of 64 assignments change or the greedy table's overall panel
edge vs v0.4.2 is <$1.5k mean cash, the measured-routing premise dies here — fall back
to the day-27 endgame audit (Step 3) as the v0.4.3 content and stop claiming class-edge.

### Step 2 — v0.4.3 = v0.4.2 with the measured table (days 3–4, 1 submission)

Mechanical, low-risk build via a `scripts/build_v043.py` in the `build_v041.py` mould:

- replace `_EXP_SHOP_ROUTES` (and the YARN half of `_router`) with `logs/v043_draw_table.csv`;
- touch nothing else — no new layers, no tape edits, no opening changes. The diff should
  be one table + the version string. This keeps the offline gate *clean*: any delta is
  attributable to the table.
- Gates (same bar as v0.4.1, against the **v0.4.2** incumbent):
  1. head-to-head ≥60% on ≥100 paired seeds, and **draw-stratified**: no pair-class
     regression vs incumbent (the new `logs/v043_draw_table.csv` per-pair evidence
     must agree with the held-out run);
  2. zero failed HIRE/BUY vs incumbent (`scripts/analyse_market_fills.py`);
  3. `rank_ladder.py --require-perfect` 10/10;
  4. `submit.py` pre-flight GREEN (allowlist, cold-start < 1s, size ≤ 100 MB);
  5. ties allowed only on pairs where the table keeps the incumbent's tape.
- Submit once, then **leave it alone** — per the double-submission lesson, push the
  git tag *before* pushing main, and do not manually submit when the release workflow
  will fire.

### Step 3 — the day-27 endgame audit (parallel, days 1–4, offline)

The one structural code path we never re-validated against engine 1.32.7: `step>=648`
switches *every* draw to route 2 (the endgame tape). The metacounter shares it, so it is
not a differentiator — but the 1.32.7 price-curve changes (CARROT hinge, scarcity
spikes) plausibly changed which endgame tape is best, and the whole class inherits the
same possibly-stale choice. A/B route 2 vs the top-3 endgame candidates (routes 1, 2, 5)
on ≥60 paired seeds across a draw-stratified panel; if route 2 loses, swapping the
day-27 target is a one-line change to fold into v0.4.3 before submission. If it wins,
we have measured that our endgame is class-parity and can stop suspecting it.

### Step 4 — live read + the midgame reset decision (days 5–8)

- v0.4.3 live read at ~2.5 days: converged WR vs the 37.5%/30.8%/29.4% band
  (`scripts/calculate_converged_winrate.py v0.4.3`, n≈35+ per quartile). Success bar:
  converged WR clearly above ~45% — i.e. outside the noise floor the identical-agent
  spread just measured. Anything inside 30–40% is parity again and we stop shipping
  table tweaks.
- If v0.4.3 confirms a real edge (≥45%): the Phase-3 midgame reset becomes worth its
  cost — the shop draw is fully observable as days 3/6/9/… unlock, so future product
  prices are predictable per-draw; with a *measured* per-draw portfolio to route
  between, a day-12 re-weighting of the remaining tape is the compounding step the
  3000-class is likely running. Prototype it offline against v0.4.3 as the incumbent.
- If v0.4.3 is parity: lock v0.4.3 (or v0.4.2 if the table regresses), spend the
  remaining slots on one clean endgame A/B if Step 3 found a winner, and hold.

## What this proposal deliberately does not do

- **No new runtime layers.** Five live failures were shipped on "wins the panel" deltas
  that bundled a layer; v0.4.3's diff is a table, so the live read measures exactly one
  hypothesis.
- **No pot-product module.** Phase 1 re-spy measured the mid-field already farms
  TOMATO/CARROT/EGG (60–85% of seats); the additive-pot premise is dead as a
  differentiator and the chassis tapes already include it.
- **No route mutation/synthesis on the tapes.** Exhausted by measurement pre-pivot;
  the per-draw *selection* is the only selection left, and it is over tapes authored
  for this engine.
- **No shipping on aggregate deltas alone.** The 120-seed aggregate hid the noise
  floor we measured today; every gate from now on is draw-stratified, and the live
  converged WR against the identical-agent band is the only trusted acceptance signal.

## Honest expected value

The per-draw table is worth maybe +$1–3k mean cash per peer game if the EXP240
assignments were meaningfully mistuned for 1.32.7 — the same order as v0.4.1's +$1.0k
gate edge, which was live-neutral at the band level but is exactly the kind of margin
that decides peer coin-flips. Rating-wise: parity→+150 if several assignments flip
with real edges; the compounding step (midgame reset over a measured portfolio) is
where +300–600 would come from, and Step 1's table is its prerequisite either way.
The distance from 2590 to the ~3000 class is most likely not one table — but this is
the only measured, tooling-backed lever left inside the class, and its failure mode
(gates veto, one submission spent) is cheap.
